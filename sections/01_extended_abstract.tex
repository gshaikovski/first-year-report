The open problem addressed in this work is understanding and improving the learning algorithms and methods that allow artificial neural networks, specifically sequence models with Transformer architecture \citep{vaswaniAttentionAllYou2017,radfordLanguageModelsAre}, to solve complex goal-oriented problems that require optimal decisions or step-by-step solutions.
Such problems are characterised by an initial state, a goal state or a desired outcome, legal transition rules between states, and restrictions on these transitions \citep{condellProblemSolvingTechniques2010,reedProblemSolving2023}.
The ability to solve them with constrained cognitive resources is one of the hallmarks of natural intelligence.
In artificial intelligence, heuristic-based state-space search is the seminal method that addresses this problem: a traversal of a graph of states under a transition rule and an evaluation function that prunes the search tree to make the procedure tractable \citep{hartFormalBasisHeuristic1968,newell1972human,russel2010}.
